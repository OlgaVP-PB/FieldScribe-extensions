🖼️ FieldScribe Photo Matcher

Links your field photos to FieldScribe samples by the moment each was taken - using the photo's built-in EXIF time (and GPS). Everything runs in your browser; no photo is uploaded anywhere.

1FieldScribe backup

In FieldScribe, tap Back up all data and load that JSON file here. It carries each sample's ID, time and GPS.

2Your photos

Select the photos from this collection (ideally taken on the same phone as the records). They are read on your device only.

3Matching settings

A photo matches a sample if taken within this many minutes of it.
Nudge if photo and record clocks differ (time zone or camera drift).
Prefer the closest sample in space, not just time.
